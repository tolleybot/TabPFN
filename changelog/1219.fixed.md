Fix the in-memory model cache serving a model that had been moved or cast out from under it. The cache hands its entry out by reference and the estimator then places that instance in place, so fitting on one device (or precision) and then another broke the earlier estimator with `Placeholder storage has not been allocated on MPS device!` or `mat1 and mat2 must have the same dtype, but got Float and Half`. The target devices and forced inference dtype are now part of the cache key, and moving a fitted estimator retires the entries it held. Comparing the bar-distribution borders of two models no longer requires them to be on the same device.
