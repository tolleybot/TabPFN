The in-memory cache of built models is now on by default, sized to hold a classifier and a regressor. Repeat fits of the same checkpoint skip reconstruction and, on GPU, re-transferring the weights: a repeat fit of the 213 MB v3 checkpoint drops from 0.105 s to 0.003 s on CPU and 0.215 s to 0.004 s on MPS. It also fixes the cache serving a model that an earlier fit had already moved to another device or cast to another precision. The cache pins the weights it holds, costing about 213 MB extra for a classifier-plus-regressor workload; set `TABPFN_MODEL_CACHE_SIZE=0` to restore the previous behaviour, or to another positive integer to resize it.
